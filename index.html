How to get your file from Spotify
  1. Open spotify.com/account/privacy in a browser and sign in. This works on a phone too, but not inside the Spotify app.
  2. Scroll to Download your data and tick Extended streaming history. The basic “Account data” only covers about a year and has no album names.
  3. Click Request data, then open the email Spotify sends you and confirm. Nothing starts until you do.
  4. Wait for a second email with your download link. Spotify says up to 30 days, but it often arrives within a few days.
  5. Download the zip and drop it above. Don’t unzip it. The link only works for a limited time, so download it soon after the email arrives.

Your file is read inside this browser tab and is never uploaded. Only the track, artist, time and play length are used. The IP addresses in the export are ignored.
